Design annotations

read once
  1. Sync Ring - change ABW in the topbar. Every card bound to weight pulses a ring + traveling dot for 1.2s. Confirms cross-module recompute.
  2. Sticky result strip - once a calculation resolves, the primary dose pins to top of the card and survives scroll.
  3. Quick grid - empty search state replaces with one-tap tiles for the 6 most common drugs. No typing required for the common path.
  4. 2-tap Copy EHR - clicking Copy arms a Confirm pill (red widening). Clicking Confirm executes. Avoids mis-tap on irreversible action.
  5. Verify line - every hero result renders the math underneath (e.g. 12.5 mg/kg x 14.0 kg = 175 mg) so clinicians can audit the calc at a glance.
  6. Three themes - click Theme in the topbar to cycle light to dark to mono. Mono is grayscale-only with luminance-ordered severity (pure white = danger).
  7. Focus - every interactive element has a 3px solid accent focus ring. Tab through to verify.
  8. Reduced motion - if your OS has reduced-motion enabled, sync ring collapses to a static outline and dot (CSS only).
